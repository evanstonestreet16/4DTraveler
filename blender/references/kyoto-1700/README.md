# Kyoto 1700 reconstruction references

Checked 12 September 2026. The Kyoto images are an original procedural reconstruction from project-authored geometry and material parameters. The sources below inform factual constraints; no third-party photograph, map tile, scan, texture, or model is incorporated. Links to architectural records are research references, not blanket permission to reuse their illustrations.

## Geographic anchor check

Use meters with `+X` east and `+Z` south. The origin is a representative Nijō Castle place pin, not the surveyed position of the Ninomaru courtyard camera. Geographic pins constrain the overview; ground-level panoramas use their own local authoring coordinates.

The check uses `X = R × cos(mean latitude) × longitude difference` and `Z = R × (origin latitude − latitude)`, with angles in radians and `R = 6,371,008.8 m`. The plan’s rounded anchors, Kiyomizu `[3350, 2150]` and Nishiki `[1500, 1000]`, agree within about 22 m and 4 m respectively; retain them. These are general place pins, not a 1700 survey. Marker heights, basin relief, and the representative Nishiki segment remain authored choices. Map viewport centers were not mistaken for place pins; the castle access map’s offset center is not the origin.
