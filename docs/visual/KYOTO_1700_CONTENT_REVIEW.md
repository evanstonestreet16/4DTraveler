## Visual reference handoff

The designation record describes Tozamurai as eight bays by eight, and Kurumayose as five by three, both single story with irimoya roofs. These are structural bay counts, not a universal conversion to meters. The castle’s [conservation report](https://nijo-jocastle.city.kyoto.lg.jp/wp-content/themes/nijojo/assets/pdf/introduction/research/research_260227_2.pdf) is a further measured-plan reference located during research, not a reviewed or licensed asset source.

The temple describes the stage at nearly 13 meters high and roughly 200 square meters, with 18 support pillars and penetrating rails. Its Main Hall uses a hipped cypress-bark roof. These measurements constrain broad massing; exact surfaces and a full circa-1700 landscape require separate evidence.
